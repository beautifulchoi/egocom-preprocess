Important Interpretation

The current manifest_mm.jsonl build filters any source window overlapping a chunk where chunk_attempts has conflict_segments > 0. This can remove rows even when the scene-level mapping summary says fallback_status: resolved_without_conflict and conflict_segments: 0.

Example: day_1__con_1__part4 has selected_chunk: 2 and overall resolved_without_conflict, but chunk 1 has chunk_attempts.conflict_segments: 2. The MM manifest removes source windows from chunk 1.
